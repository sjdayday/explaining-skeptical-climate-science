Benestad is wrong to say that the ice core data violates the condition "f(x) is
bounded". The measured dataset is, obviously, bounded.
Furthermore, the Dirichlet conditions apply to a function f(x) defined for all values of a
continuous variable x. It makes little sense to try to apply them to a discrete
observational dataset.

[Project note: this concerns the ESDD draft's treatment of the Humlum et al. ice-core
